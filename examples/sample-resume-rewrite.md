# Sample Resume Rewrite

## Before

```text
Responsible for AI product features and user experience optimization. Worked with engineering and algorithm teams to improve product experience.
```

## After

```text
Owned the 0-1 roadmap for an AI knowledge platform across App and Web, turning a hardware recording entry point into an AI information workflow: capture, transcription, summarization, knowledge base, and AI query.
```

## Why It Works

- Shows ownership: `Owned the 0-1 roadmap`
- Names the product object: `AI knowledge platform`
- Explains the mechanism: `capture, transcription, summarization, knowledge base, AI query`
- Connects hardware, software, and AI value

## Another Example

### Before

```text
Designed Agent and Skill Market related features.
```

### After

```text
Owned Agent Template and embedded Skill Market modules, turning repeatable tasks into configurable intelligent workflows and enabling users to discover, add, and invoke skills inside agent scenarios.
```

## Rewrite Pattern

```text
ownership + product object + mechanism + user/business/model value
```
