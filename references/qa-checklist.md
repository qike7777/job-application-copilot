# Final Application QA Checklist

Use this reference before final delivery or when reviewing an exported resume.

## Content QA

- Target role is obvious in the header and summary.
- Top half contains the most relevant evidence for the JD.
- Every major JD criterion has visible proof or an intentional omission.
- Ownership level is clear: owner, module owner, contributor, designer, growth PM.
- AI/Agent terms are tied to product work, not floating as buzzwords.
- Metrics are accurate or written as metric categories when exact numbers are unavailable.
- No invented claims, fake tools, or exaggerated scope.

## ATS / Recruiter Scan QA

- Text extraction preserves contact info, titles, company names, and bullets.
- Important keywords appear in plain text, not only images.
- URLs are selectable/clickable or separately visible.
- Company names, dates, and roles are consistent.
- The resume is one page unless the target market expects otherwise.

## Visual QA

- No text overlap, clipping, or cramped last section.
- Header, summary, section titles, and company titles have clear hierarchy.
- Line heights are readable for Chinese and mixed English terms.
- Tags align consistently and do not pull attention away from body content.
- Section dividers help scanning without making the page feel fragmented.
- Portrait, if present, is cropped cleanly and does not dominate.

## Language QA

- Replace filler words: `负责相关工作`, `参与项目`, `协助推进`, `提升体验`.
- Prefer concrete verbs: `定义`, `主导`, `设计`, `搭建`, `上线`, `迭代`, `验证`, `衡量`.
- Check English capitalization: `Agent`, `Skill Market`, `Roadmap`, `API`, `MCP`, `A/B`.
- Keep separator style consistent: `｜`, `/`, `-`.
- Avoid overly long Chinese sentences; split if a bullet carries multiple ideas.

## Link QA

- Portfolio opens.
- GitHub opens and shows relevant work.
- Product links open.
- Figma-exported PDF links still work after export.

## Final Response

When returning a review:

1. Start with the verdict: ready / almost ready / needs major revision.
2. List highest-impact fixes first.
3. Provide exact replacement wording for any content issue.
4. Note what was checked: text extraction, visual render, links, JD fit.
5. Keep the answer concise unless the user asks for a full audit.
